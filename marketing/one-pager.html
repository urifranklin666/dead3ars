Scope & Authorization Boundary

Every Blackwire lab runs exclusively inside an air-gapped, ephemeral KVM sandbox. No module targets a third party. Coursework explicitly teaches compliance with the CFAA (18 U.S.C. § 1030), UK Computer Misuse Act 1990, and EU Directive 2013/40/EU.
